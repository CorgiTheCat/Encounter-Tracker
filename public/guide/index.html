This page is the guide, not the tracker. The interactive tracker runs inside an Owlbear Rodeo room with a Scene open.
01
ONE-TIME SETUP

Install the extension

  1. Open Owlbear Rodeo and go to Extensions.
  2. Choose +, then Add a custom extension.
  3. Copy the complete installation link below into Install Link and choose Add.
  4. Enable Encounter Tracker in your room, open a Scene, and select the dragon icon to open it.
Select this field and copy the entire URL. On a phone, press and hold to select.
02
BUILD YOUR PARTY

Add combatants

From Scene Tokens

Select one or several Tokens on the Scene. Open their context menu and choose Add to Encounter. All selected eligible Tokens are added.

This links each entry to its Scene Token for name updates and optional camera follow.

From your Assets

Choose Add from Assets or the Add combatant card, then select an image from the Asset picker.

An Asset-only entry has no linked Scene Token, so camera follow will skip it.

Set each combatant's Initiative and side (Player or Monster). Choose Sort Initiative to arrange the order.

03
LET THE STORY UNFOLD

Run an encounter

  1. Choose Start Encounter to switch your view to the encounter bar.
  2. The highlighted, larger card is the current combatant. Choose Next › to advance if you have turn permission.
  3. After the last combatant, the tracker moves to the next round.
  4. Use the gear to return to the editor, and Back to Encounter Bar to return without stopping the encounter.
ROUNDCurrent round
TURNPosition in the roster
TIME6 seconds per round

The time display uses the current round number × 6 seconds: Round 1 shows 6 seconds; Round 10 shows 1 minute. It is not a real-time stopwatch.

The × closes your tracker window. Stop Encounter stops the shared encounter; it is not the same as closing the window.
MAKE IT YOUR OWN

Portraits & names

Select a card's portrait and choose a replacement Asset. Your full-art portrait changes only in the tracker—the Token image on the Scene stays unchanged.

For a linked Token, its Scene text label takes priority over its image/Asset name. For example, a Token originally named “Image” becomes “Lucky Jack” in the tracker when you set its Scene text to “Lucky Jack”.

SHARED PARTY, PERSONAL VIEW

Your controls

Eye · Camera follow

Turn the eye on to center your view on the active linked Token. A green outline means it is enabled. Turn it off to explore freely.

This setting is personal. Missing or unlinked Tokens leave your camera unchanged.

Size · S / M / L

Choose a size for your encounter bar. Open the dropdown, release the mouse, then select the size you want.

Your editor/bar view and camera preference do not switch your friends' views.

Player turns · GM control

OFF: only the GM can advance turns. ON: players can also advance turns. Players without permission can still open and view the tracker, but do not see the Next control.

These controls are extension behavior, not a separate server-enforced security boundary.

DOWN, BUT NOT FORGOTTEN

Dead & revive

In the editor, choose Mark dead on a combatant's card. The portrait becomes grayscale and receives a DEAD label. Choose Revive to restore it.

This is a manual status marker, not automatic HP detection. It does not delete the Token.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF

Saved encounters

The roster, current turn, round and Dead status are shared using Owlbear Scene/Room metadata. Personal view settings stay local to your browser.

The GM can choose Clear all combatants and confirm to remove the roster for everyone. This does not delete Scene Tokens or Assets.

A new Scene or room is not a backup of another encounter. Avoid simultaneous roster edits, and check your saved encounter before a session.
WHEN THE DICE DON'T COOPERATE

Troubleshooting

“Failed to fetch” during installation

Use the complete HTTPS manifest link above. Opening it directly should show JSON, not a 404 or sign-in page. If the problem persists, report your browser and the error to the maintainer.

The tracker is connecting or blank

Check your network connection and open a Scene in Owlbear. The tracker retries its connection automatically a limited number of times. Use Retry or Reload tracker when offered. Opening the website outside Owlbear is not interactive.

I cannot see the Next button

Ask the GM to check Player turns. With that switch off, only the GM can advance turns.

The eye is enabled but the camera does not move

Camera follow needs a linked Token still present in the Scene. Asset-only entries and missing Tokens are skipped. Add the Scene Token through Add to Encounter to link it.

How do I report a bug?

Open a GitHub Issue with the steps, expected result, actual result, and your browser/device. Please do not include private room links, credentials or player information.
